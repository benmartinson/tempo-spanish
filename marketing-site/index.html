Why Tempo

A different kind of language app.

No streaks to guilt-trip you. No cartoon owls. Just real video, real speakers, and real practice — the way you'd actually learn if you lived there.

Real content

Learn from creators you'd actually watch.

Browse 100's of videos from curated YouTube channels organized by topic and difficulty. Stories, history, film reviews, current events, vlogs — pick what interests you and start practicing.

  • Beginner, intermediate, and advanced channels
  • New videos added daily
  • Request your favorite channel be added
Practice by Shadowing

Repeat after them.

Tempo plays each segment, then hands the mic to you. Speak along, submit your take, and learn from your mistakes. Your tongue becomes familiar with the sounds and rhythms of the language.

  • The video will play along while you speak
  • Words will highlight as you go to keep the pace
  • One-tap re-record on every segment
Adaptive difficulty

Hide as much — or as little — as you need.

Hint slider lets you mask words on the transcript, from a gentle nudge to full memorization. Tempo auto-adjusts based on the segment difficulty so it's always just hard enough.

  • Auto-select hints per segment difficulty
  • Per-segment override whenever you want
  • Develop better vocabulary recall over time
Instant feedback

Know exactly where you tripped up.

After submitting your attempt Tempo scores your accuracy and highlights the words you missed, swapped, or mumbled. It's the feedback loop that makes shadowing actually work.

  • Word-level diff against the target sentence
  • Identify which words you struggled to pronounce correctly
  • Replay your own recording to compare
Voice control

Hands-free practice

Tempo listens. Say "repeat" to hear it again, "slower" to drop the playback speed, or "record" to start practicing — without ever touching the screen.

  • Voice-trigger every action: play, pause, repeat, record, submit
  • Just your earbuds — your phone never has to leave your pocket
  • Practice while walking the dog, on a hike, or in the car
