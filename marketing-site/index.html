What is Tempo?

Tempo is a mobile app for learning Spanish through shadowing — repeating aloud what a native speaker says, sentence by sentence, with immediate feedback on your accuracy.

Practice with real YouTube videos, adjust difficulty to hide words or add hints, and build fluency at your own pace.
